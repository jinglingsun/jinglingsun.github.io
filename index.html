About me

Jingling Sun is a Lecturer at the School of Information and Software Engineering, University of Electronic Science and Technology of China (UESTC), China (2023–present).

Previously, she received her Ph.D. and B.S. degrees from East China Normal University (ECNU), Shanghai, China (2014–2023), supervised by Prof. Geguang Pu and Prof. Ting Su.

She also visited Singapore Management University (SMU) from 2022 to 2023, advised by Prof. Jun Sun.
